We are Ultronic, a student-led FIRST Tech Challenge team dedicated to pushing the boundaries of robotics and inspiring the next generation of STEM leaders.

Learn More Sponsor Us

Driven by Passion & Precision

Based in Sarasota, Florida, Ultronic is a community FTC team with a mission to create meaningful impact on and off the field through student leadership and technical excellence.

Engineering First

We follow a CAD-first, iterative design process. Every subsystem is prototyped, tested, and refined to ensure peak performance and reliability under pressure.

Reliable Software

Our code is built on clean architecture and rigorous testing. From autonomous pathing to sensor fusion, we write software that performs consistently and predictably.

Community Focused

We believe in the power of sharing knowledge. We mentor FLL teams, host local workshops, and share our journey openly to inspire others in STEM.
