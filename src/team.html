Who We Are

We’re Ultronic Robotics #23979, a student-led team competing in the FIRST Tech Challenge. Our mission is to innovate boldly, compete at the highest level, and make STEM education accessible to everyone. We bring together passionate students from across Bradenton, Lakewood Ranch, and Sarasota who thrive on creativity, collaboration, and community impact.



Our Mission

To innovate boldly, compete with integrity, and expand access to STEM locally and globally.

Meet the Members

FIRST Dean's List Award

The FIRST Dean’s List Award honors select student leaders who embody the ideals of FIRST — dedication, leadership, technical expertise, and a passion for inspiring others.

Global Winner

#FIRSTLikeAGirl

With more than half of our team being women, we at Ultronic are proud to support #FIRSTLikeAGirl - a movement that empowers young women to lead, innovate, and break barriers in STEM.

Our female leaders design, code, build, and strategize with brilliance and confidence, proving every day that girls belong everywhere robots, and big ideas, are being built.

"Being on this team has shown me that my voice matters and my skills can make a real impact, both on the robot and in my community."

- Natalie
