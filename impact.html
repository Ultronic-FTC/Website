NASA STELLA Program

COASTWISE: Partnering with NASA

Through the COASTWISE program (Community Observations and Analysis using Spectroscopic Techniques and Wetlands Imaging to foster Stewardship of the Environment), Ultronic members work alongside NASA engineers using STELLA instruments to collect and analyze spectroscopic data from Florida's wetland ecosystems. Our team was interviewed by NASA engineer Michael Taylor about the impact of working with NASA and how our engagement with COASTWISE has shaped our approach to science, data, and real-world research.

Students learn to calculate NASA's NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index) from their own field measurements, directly connecting hands-on local observations to global satellite monitoring missions like Landsat. This partnership has opened new pathways into Earth science, environmental monitoring, and data analysis — extending our team's impact far beyond the competition field.
