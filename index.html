Welcome to Ultronic

A high-performing, student-led FIRST Tech Challenge team from Sarasota, Florida. We design, code, and compete with passion and precision. Not just to win, but to inspire. Every day, we push the boundaries of robotics while empowering students of all ages to discover their potential in STEM.


Through innovation, mentorship, and community engagement, we are proving that when students are given the tools to create, they don’t just build robots. They build confidence, purpose, and a better future.

What is FIRST?

FIRST (For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology) is the world’s leading youth robotics community, a movement that transforms curiosity into capability and innovation into impact.

Founded in 1989 by inventor Dean Kamen, FIRST equips students with the technical, creative, and leadership skills that fuel their future careers and inspire them to make a difference.


Across more than 100 countries, over 3.2 million young people have experienced the life-changing power of FIRST. With 700,000+ students currently involved, the program is proven to build 21st-century skills like problem-solving, communication, and resilience, while increasing motivation to pursue STEM careers.
