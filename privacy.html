Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 25, 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how FTRunner ("the App") handles your information.

Information We Collect

The App may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Your FTC team number
  • Your FIRST API username
  • Your FIRST API key

How Your Information is Stored

All data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your information on external servers. Your data never leaves your device.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer your data to any third parties. Since all data remains on your device, we have no access to it.

Third-Party Services

The App connects to the following third-party services:

  • FIRST API - Using credentials you provide. Your use of the FIRST API is subject to FIRST's own terms and privacy policies.
  • FTCScout API - To retrieve team and event statistics. Your use is subject to FTCScout's privacy policy.

Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device using standard device storage. The security of your data depends on your device's security settings.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. You can delete your data at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Connect page.
