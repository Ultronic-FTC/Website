Ultronic, Klutch Robotics and Mysteria are excited to welcome FTC teams from across Florida for a winter break gathering focused on camaraderie, collaboration, and robotics fun. We plan to bring together 12–14 teams for a relaxed, low-pressure event where teams can connect, support one another, and enjoy plenty of gameplay.

This is intentionally a FUN, community-building event—designed to foster relationships among teams from across our state who may not otherwise have the opportunity to meet. If you are looking to connect with great teams, get extra drive time in a game setting, and enjoy a collaborative robotics experience, this event is for you.

We are incredibly grateful to the Suncoast Science Center for generously donating their space to host this event.

Important Information

Due to space limitations, participation is limited to 12–14 teams, and we kindly ask that each team bring no more than five team members so we can accommodate as many teams as possible while remaining within capacity and safety guidelines.

We look forward to an exciting and memorable event with Florida's FTC community!
